# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Match AI responses to the current meeting notes and record state.
- Preserve unreadable browser storage and detect conflicting tab changes before saving.
- Group agenda items once and separate unconfirmed suggestions in follow-up drafts.

- Standalone meeting tracker with independent workspaces and local browser storage.
- AI prompt generation and response validation, with review before changes are applied.
- Action and decision tracking, meeting history and deadline alerts.
- Agenda and follow-up drafts, CSV export and JSON backup.
- Fictional three-meeting demonstration and example files.
