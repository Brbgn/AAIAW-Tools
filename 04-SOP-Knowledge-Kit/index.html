How the example was built

01 / EXAMINE

Review the historical expense SOP, Finance announcement, team chat and process owner decision. Record contradictions with source IDs.

02 / DOCUMENT

Draft the approved fictional process using numbered actions, boundaries and a decision table. Preserve who decided what and when.

03 / FIND AND MAINTAIN

Register each SOP in the ledger. Use stable links, category tags, owners, effective dates, and change history.
