# SOP Knowledge Kit | AAIAW #04

This learning kit uses fictional source records to demonstrate how to document a process, maintain an evidence trail, and register SOPs in a searchable catalog.

**[Open the interactive SOP catalog](https://brbgn.github.io/AAIAW-Tools/04-SOP-Knowledge-Kit/)**

## Explore the example

1. Read the [fictional source pack](Source_Pack/) for the expense-reimbursement process. One chat message incorrectly suggests that an approval change applies to all locations.
2. Compare it with [FIN-001](SOPs/FIN-001.html). The final example identifies the Region A pilot period, rejects the broader chat interpretation and marks unresolved boundary questions for owner review.
3. Open [the master ledger](SOP_Master_Ledger.xlsx). Filter by category, relevant tool, keywords, owner, or review date. Its links point to the example SOPs in this repository. The table filters and dashboard follow the catalog rows. Entry validation helps identify duplicate IDs and standardize status values; pasted entries still require review.
4. Use the [SOP template](SOP_Template.docx), [AI workflow prompts](AI_SOP_Workflow_Prompts.txt), and [evidence register](Evidence_Register.csv) to practice with fictional or otherwise approved source material.
5. Review [the example change history](Change_History.csv) to see how ownership, source decisions, and effective dates can be recorded.

## How to manage a real SOP library

- Keep one authoritative approved SOP per process. Store the official document in the organization's controlled Google Drive or SharePoint location.
- Give each SOP a stable ID, accountable owner, approval status, effective date, and next review date.
- Use native document headings, meaningful filenames, and metadata. Maintain one master directory with links, rather than copying procedures into the directory.
- Preserve actual approvals and changes in the organization’s version history and governance records. A spreadsheet does not by itself prove who approved a document.
- When AI can access the company repository, ask it to link to and cite the approved SOP. Verify responses against the original document. A spreadsheet ledger **does not automatically create or configure an AI search index**.

## Responsible use

All company examples, approvals, rules, dates and procedures in this kit are fictional. They are provided for learning; real workplace controls require authorization. No confidential workplace records are required.

Do not upload sensitive chat exports, email threads, employee data, customer information, or internal policies into an unapproved AI service. Use only authorized data and company-approved tools. A process owner must verify and approve real SOPs before publication.

This kit is educational and does not configure Google Drive, Microsoft SharePoint, Microsoft 365 Copilot, or Gemini indexing. Search quality depends on actual access permissions and enterprise configuration.
