**Approved interpretation for this fictional scenario**

- Only eligible **taxi expense claims** in **Region A** qualify for the pilot approval exception.
- The exception applies when the claim is **€50 or less**, inclusive. Values above €50 require manager approval.
- Region B claims and all non-taxi expense categories retain manager approval.
- All claims require a readable receipt, portal submission within 30 calendar days, and Finance eligibility review.
- If Finance returns a claim, the employee corrects the existing claim and resubmits it through the portal; the claim then follows the same routing rules.
- Managers must not approve their own claims. Those claims are routed to the manager's designated approver according to the organization's separate delegation policy. The delegation policy itself is not included in this example.
- The pilot is scheduled to conclude on January 31, 2027. Any extension requires a new approved decision; do not assume renewal.
- Finance Operations will review FIN-001 by January 15, 2027.
