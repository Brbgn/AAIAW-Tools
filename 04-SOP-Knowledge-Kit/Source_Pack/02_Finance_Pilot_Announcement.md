# Finance announcement · Taxi reimbursement pilot

**Northstar Services (fictional demonstration)**  
**From:** Finance Operations  
**Date:** November 4, 2026  
**Subject:** Region A taxi claim approval pilot  
**Source reference:** SRC-02

Beginning **November 16, 2026**, eligible employee taxi expenses **up to and including €50** in **Region A** do not require manager approval. The pilot is scheduled through **January 31, 2027**, with a review before that date.

Employees must still submit claims through the Expenses portal within 30 calendar days and attach readable receipts. Finance remains responsible for eligibility and receipt review. Claims above €50 and claims from Region B continue through manager approval. Other expense categories are unchanged.

The announcement does not specify how claims rejected by Finance are resubmitted or whether the pilot will continue after January 31.
