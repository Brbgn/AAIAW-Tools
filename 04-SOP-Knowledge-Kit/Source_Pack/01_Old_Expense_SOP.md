## Expense submission rules

1. An employee submits an expense claim through the Expenses portal within 30 calendar days of the expense date.
2. A readable receipt is required for every claim.
3. All expense categories, including taxi travel, require manager approval before Finance review.
4. Finance checks the receipt and eligibility, then either confirms the claim or returns it for correction.
5. Returned claims can be resubmitted with corrected information.

**Scope:** Regions A and B. **Process owner:** Finance Operations.  
**Source reference:** SRC-01.
