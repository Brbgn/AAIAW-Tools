# HR workflow note · Employee onboarding

**Northstar Services (fictional demonstration)**  
**Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Source reference:** SRC-06

HR creates the onboarding case after the signed offer and confirmed start date. The hiring manager records the role-specific equipment and access needs. IT provisions approved accounts and equipment using the onboarding case. On the first working day, the manager confirms access and completes the introductory checklist with the employee. Unresolved access or equipment issues are logged on the case and escalated to IT Service Desk. HR closes onboarding only after the day-one checks have been recorded.

**Owner:** People Operations. **Approver:** People Operations Lead. **Effective date:** September 16, 2026. **Review date:** March 31, 2027.
