HR-003 · New employee onboarding

Coordinate onboarding cases, equipment, and first-day readiness.

1. Purpose and scope

HR, hiring managers, and IT teams responsible for a new employee’s first-day setup.

2. Before you begin

  • Signed offer and confirmed start date are recorded.
  • An onboarding case can be created.

3. Procedure

  1. 1. Open the case
    HR creates the onboarding case once the offer and start date are confirmed.
  2. 2. Define requirements
    The hiring manager records equipment and role-specific access needs.
  3. 3. Provision access
    IT provisions approved accounts and equipment against the onboarding case.
  4. 4. Perform day-one checks
    The manager confirms the employee can use required access and completes the introductory checklist.
  5. 5. Close the case
    HR closes the case only after the day-one checks are recorded.

4. Decision guide

ConditionRequired routeAdditional requirement
Equipment or access missingKeep case openLog issue and escalate to IT Service Desk
Day-one checks recorded and completeHR may close caseRetain case record

5. Exceptions and escalation

  • Unresolved access or equipment issue: record the issue and escalate to IT.
  • Unconfirmed start date: do not open the onboarding case under this process.
