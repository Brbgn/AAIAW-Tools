IT-002 · Account access reset

Request a routine account reset through approved IT support channels.

CategoryIT
Process ownerIT Service Desk
StatusDemonstration / fictional
Version1.0
Effective date2026-10-21
ApproverIT Operations Lead
Review by2027-03-31
SourcesSRC-05

1. Purpose and scope

Employees requesting restoration of their own work account access.

2. Before you begin

  • Access to the approved IT Service Desk request channel.
  • Identity verification must follow the separate approved IT policy.

3. Procedure

  1. 1. Submit a ticket
    Open an account access reset request through IT Service Desk.
  2. 2. Verify identity
    IT completes the approved identity-verification procedure before any reset.
  3. 3. Perform authorized reset
    If verification succeeds and no compromise indicators exist, IT initiates the authorized reset.
  4. 4. Confirm access
    The employee confirms access through the standard sign-in process. IT records the outcome in the ticket.

4. Decision guide

ConditionRequired routeAdditional requirement
Identity verification successful; no risk indicatorProceed with authorized resetRecord result in ticket
Verification fails or account appears compromisedDo not perform routine resetEscalate to Security team

5. Exceptions and escalation

  • Verification method unavailable: follow the separate identity-verification policy; do not improvise.
  • Potential compromise: Security escalation takes precedence over routine reset.
