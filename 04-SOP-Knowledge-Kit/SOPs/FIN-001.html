FIN-001 · Expense reimbursement

Submit eligible expenses and determine when manager approval is required.

1. Purpose and scope

Employees in Regions A and B using the fictional Expenses portal. The Region A pilot applies only to eligible taxi expenses.

2. Before you begin

  • Expense date, amount in euros, category, and region are known.
  • A readable receipt is available.
  • The claim is submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.

3. Procedure

  1. 1. Prepare the claim
    Collect the receipt, date, amount, region, and expense category.
  2. 2. Enter the expense
    Open the Expenses portal and create a claim with accurate details.
  3. 3. Route for approval
    For eligible Region A taxi claims of €50 or less, skip manager approval. All other claims route to the manager or designated approver.
  4. 4. Finance review
    Finance verifies the receipt and expense eligibility. This check applies to every claim, including the pilot exception.
  5. 5. Complete or correct
    If Finance returns a claim, correct the existing claim and resubmit it. Apply the same approval route again. If Finance confirms it, the claim moves to the usual reimbursement processing.

4. Decision guide

Region A · eligible taxi · above €50Manager approval requiredFinance review follows

5. Exceptions and escalation

  • Unreadable or missing receipt: return the claim for correction.
  • Unverified eligibility: Finance returns the claim for clarification.
  • Failed self-approval check: route to the designated approver.
  • Pilot after January 31, 2027: do not assume continuation without a new approved decision.

6. Control and change history

v2.0: Added Region A taxi pilot up to and including €50; clarified routing, Finance checks, and resubmissions. Source DEC-FIN-2026-11-12.
